Windows Shortcut

Create a desktop shortcut on Windows:

  1. Create a file named Valiyakath.url.
  2. Paste the content below into it:
[InternetShortcut] URL=https://valiyakath.vercel.app/

Double-click the .url file anytime to open your chat.

📱

iOS (iPhone / iPad)

Add to your Home Screen for instant app-like access:

  1. Open Safari and go to valiyakath.vercel.app.
  2. Log in to your account once.
  3. Tap the Share icon (bottom bar).
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Name it Valiyakath Chat and tap Add.
🤖

Android Shortcut

Add to your Android Home Screen:

  1. Open Chrome and visit valiyakath.vercel.app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) top right.
  4. Tap Add to Home screen.
  5. Tap Add to confirm.

Note: Your session is maintained by your device's browser local storage & cookies. Logging in once keeps you signed in whenever you re-open the shortcut.
