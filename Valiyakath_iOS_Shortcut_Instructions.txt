VALIYAKATH CHAT - IOS SHORTCUT & WEB APP (PWA) SETUP
=====================================================

To create a shortcut on iOS (iPhone / iPad) that opens Valiyakath Chat and keeps you logged in:

METHOD 1: Add to Home Screen (Safari Web App) - RECOMMENDED
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Go to: https://valiyakath.vercel.app/
3. Log in to your Valiyakath chat account.
4. Tap the "Share" button (the box with an arrow pointing up at the bottom of the screen).
5. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
6. Name it "Valiyakath Chat" and tap "Add".
7. A shortcut icon will be placed on your iPhone Home Screen.
8. When you tap this icon, it opens directly into Valiyakath Chat, retaining your login session across uses.

METHOD 2: Apple Shortcuts App
-----------------------------
1. Open the "Shortcuts" app on your iOS device.
2. Tap the "+" icon to create a new shortcut.
3. Add action: "Open URL".
4. Enter `https://valiyakath.vercel.app/` as the URL.
5. Tap the share/details menu, select "Add to Home Screen".
6. Name it "Valiyakath Chat" and tap "Add".
