VALIYAKATH CHAT - ANDROID SHORTCUT & APP SETUP
==============================================

Option 1: Build & Install the Android App (APK)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Use Android Studio or GitHub Actions to build `app-debug.apk` from this repository.
2. Install the APK on your Android phone.
3. Open the app, log in to Valiyakath Chat once.
4. The app uses persistent WebView cookies and LocalStorage, keeping you signed in when re-opened.

Option 2: Browser Home Screen Shortcut (Instant, No Build Needed)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Google Chrome (or your default web browser) on your Android phone.
2. Visit: https://valiyakath.vercel.app/
3. Log in to your account.
4. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner.
5. Tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").
6. Confirm by tapping "Add".
7. A Valiyakath Chat shortcut icon is now on your home screen and will open directly into your logged-in session.
